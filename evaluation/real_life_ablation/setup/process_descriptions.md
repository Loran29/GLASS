# Process descriptions

Written from each baseline BPMN model only (`data/<dataset>/<dataset>_complex_hyperopt_holdout_after_enhanced.bpmn`):
activities, their order, choices and parallel branches. The models have no roles, so the descriptions speak of
"the staff". They contain no numbers and no hints about KPIs; the timer events of the models (the extraneous
delays) are not described. Each step is named in plain English, followed by the model's activity name in quotes.

## AcademicCredentials

The academic credentials process handles students' requests to have courses they have already taken recognised. All steps are carried out by the staff. A request starts in one of two ways: the staff either validates the request ("Validar solicitud"), or retrieves the student's information from the student system ("Traer informacion estudiante - banner") and then files the recognition request ("Radicar Solicitud Homologacion"). After filing, the request goes to validation, to the recognition of a group of courses ("Homologacion por grupo de cursos"), to cancellation, or directly to the notification of the student about the cancellation. After validation, the staff reviews the courses ("Revisar curso"), receives documents ("Recepcion de documentos"), proceeds to the recognition of a group of courses, retrieves the student's information again, cancels the request, or closes the case. After reviewing the courses, the staff either evaluates a course ("Evaluacion curso") or proceeds to the recognition of a group of courses; after receiving documents, the request proceeds to the recognition of a group of courses. After the recognition of a group of courses, the staff evaluates a course, or cancels a course ("Cancelar curso"), possibly followed by advancing the document reception ("Avanzar recepcion documentos"), or the case ends. After a course evaluation, the staff performs the final validation ("Validacion final"), followed by the sign-off that closes the process ("Visto Bueno Cierre Proceso") and, where applicable, the transfer of credits ("Transferir Creditos") and of the recognisable credits ("Transferir creditos homologables"); or the staff validates the request at the pre-recognition stage ("Validar solicitud / pre-homologacion"); or the case ends. A cancelled request ("Cancelar Solicitud") is followed by a notification to the student about the cancellation ("Notificacion estudiante cancelacion soli"). Validation, retrieving the student's information and filing the request can be repeated. There are no parallel branches.

## BPIC_2012_W

This process covers the work on loan applications at a financial institution. All steps are carried out by the staff. When an application arrives, two branches run in parallel. In one branch, the staff assesses the application for fraud ("W_Beoordelen fraude"). In the other branch, the staff may first handle the lead ("W_Afhandelen leads"); after that, the case either ends or the staff completes the application ("W_Completeren aanvraag"). After the application has been completed, the staff may call the customer about the offer ("W_Nabellen offertes"); after that, the staff may validate the application ("W_Valideren aanvraag"), and after the validation the staff may call the customer about incomplete files ("W_Nabellen incomplete dossiers"). Each of these follow-up steps can be skipped. The case ends when both branches have finished.

## BPIC_2017_W

This process covers the work on loan applications at a financial institution. All steps are carried out by the staff. A case may start with the staff handling the lead ("W_Handle leads"). The staff then completes the application ("W_Complete application"), sometimes followed by a shortened completion ("W_Shortened completion"). Next, the staff calls the customer after the offer has been sent ("W_Call after offers"). After that, the case either ends, or the staff validates the application ("W_Validate application"), after which the staff may assess potential fraud ("W_Assess potential fraud") or call the customer about incomplete files ("W_Call incomplete files") before the case ends. There are no parallel branches.
